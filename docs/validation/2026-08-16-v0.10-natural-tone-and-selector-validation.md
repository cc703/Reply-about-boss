# v0.10 Natural Tone And Selector Validation

## Scope

This iteration adds conversational job-seeker wording plus manual preset/custom tone selection. It keeps human review, manual copying, localhost-only requests, and no auto-fill/no auto-send behavior.

## Implemented

- Added `自然`, `简洁直接`, `稳妥留余地`, `积极一些`, and `自定义` controls to the in-page panel.
- Custom tone input is shown only for `自定义`, normalized to one line, capped at 80 characters, and not persisted.
- Presets ignore stale custom text. Unknown or empty tone values fall back to `自然`.
- Backend treats tone as wording-only guidance that cannot override factuality, safety, manual review, or JSON format.
- Prompt now prefers 1-3 conversational sentences and at most one practical job-seeking question.
- Local fallbacks were rewritten without formal customer-service phrases.
- Output guards now reject unconfirmed capability, interest, chat availability, attendance, interview time, invented schedule details, and malformed JSON fragments.

## Defects Found During Model QA

Anonymized DeepSeek runs exposed and then locked with regression tests:

- unconfirmed statements such as `到岗三天没问题` and `我可以接受`;
- invented personal schedules such as checking whether there are other arrangements;
- omitted-subject technical claims such as having daily Python exposure;
- chat availability claims such as `方便呀` and `可以的`;
- truncated or nested JSON displayed as draft text;
- a fallback that hard-coded four attendance days even when the recruiter stated another number.

## Final Five-Scenario Sample

| Scenario | Tone | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General greeting | `natural` | Safe local fallback asks what the role does and its intern requirements without confirming immediate availability. |
| Technical capability | `cautious` | DeepSeek drafts ask for actual task/stack requirements without claiming Python or FastAPI experience. |
| Recruiter follow-up | `concise` | One short draft asks about daily work without inventing a delayed-reply excuse or denying concerns. |
| Job details plus attendance | `positive` | Safe local fallback acknowledges the details, leaves attendance unconfirmed, and asks about team projects. |
| Interview scheduling | custom student-like tone | DeepSeek drafts state that the time needs confirmation and ask only about interview duration. |

No final sampled draft claimed unprovided technical experience, current availability, attendance compatibility, interview availability, or a reason for delayed response. No JSON fragment was shown.
